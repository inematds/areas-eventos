WHAT EACH ONE IS

The nine areas at a glance

1.1 ~20 min

AI Management: 2027, the year of managing agents

In the site menu: “2027: managing agents”

Explain why companies with agents need management, not just technology, using the 8 competencies and LOOP-R.

1.2 ~20 min

AGI-ready: Your Work Becomes Managing Agents

In the site menu: “directing agents”

Explain the difference between giving step-by-step instructions and delegating to an agent, using the ladder of intention, objective, prompt, skill, context, tools, memory, permissions, and evals.

1.3 ~20 min

Cultivated AI: you cultivate an agent; you don’t program one

In the site menu: “you don’t program it; you cultivate it”

Explain why an agent improves through the environment you cultivate, not through the model, using the eight elements, the cycle, and the kit’s five files.

2.1 ~20 min

Claude → Codex: Separate the brain from the model

In the site menu: “migrate or stay tool-agnostic”

Explain why lock-in lives in the runtime and how the Audit → Adapt → Prove → Handoff method moves project knowledge into a portable core.

2.2 ~20 min

Codex + Claude: one does the work, the other reviews it

In the site menu: “use both together”

Explain how to use Claude and Codex on the same project, covering the Use Both kit’s six levels, the routing card, and the difference between claudex and Use Both.

2.3 ~20 min

OSWork: Which Edition Is for You?

In the site menu: “from chat to your agent environment”

Explain what OSWork is, what you build with it, and which of the five editions (v2, v6.2, v6, v5, Quick) fits each person’s experience and available time.

3.1 ~20 min

RSI: AI that helps improve AI

In the site menu: “AI improvement cycles”

Explain the three levels of improvement in AI and turn an improvement idea into a verifiable test, with a recorded version and a way to roll back.

3.2 ~20 min

JEV: AI Decisions in Practice

In the site menu: “AI decisions in practice”

Explain what a structured decision with Jev is, choose between Choice, Noul, and Score, and separate what has already been observed from what still needs to be measured.

3.3 ~20 min

WebMCP: Your Site Talking to Agents

In the site menu: “sites that communicate with agents”

Explain what changes when agents visit websites, what WebMCP is, and the first concrete step: assess your own site and fix the basics.

WHAT YOU'LL TAKE AWAY

By the end, you’ll know which area to start with, without having to read all nine pages first.
